# The 28th Amendment Project

**What should Americans add to their Constitution?**

A public-facing research project inviting participants to explore constitutional provisions from around the world using [Constitute](https://www.constituteproject.org/), choose an idea they think the United States should adopt, and draft a proposed 28th Amendment.

## Pilot participant flow

1. Land on the project site.
2. Enter a short Qualtrics research instrument (consent + baseline attitudes).
3. Open Constitute and explore constitutional rights from around the world.
4. Return to the instrument and identify the provision/country that inspired the proposal.
5. Draft a proposed 28th Amendment in the participant's own words.
6. Explain why the amendment belongs in the U.S. Constitution.
7. Complete short post-experience measures.
8. View aggregate results and, where appropriate, an anonymized proposal gallery.

## Core pilot data

- anonymous participant ID
- pre-exercise constitutional attitudes
- Constitute source URL (when available)
- source country / constitution
- source provision text or topic
- proposed 28th Amendment text
- participant rationale
- post-exercise constitutional attitudes
- limited demographics / political knowledge measures as required by the research design

Raw respondent data should **not** be stored in this public repository.

## Research priorities

The first pilot is intentionally narrow:

1. What constitutional rights do Americans choose when invited to look globally for ideas?
2. Which countries and constitutions do they draw from?
3. How do participants translate foreign constitutional language into American constitutional proposals?
4. Does the exercise change openness to constitutional amendment or attitudes toward learning from other constitutions?

Later studies can add deliberation, group diversity, civility, political knowledge, experimental treatments, and behavioral search-path logging.

## Site status

The site is currently an MVP prototype. The Qualtrics launch link and live results are placeholders pending instrument design and IRB/data-handling decisions.
